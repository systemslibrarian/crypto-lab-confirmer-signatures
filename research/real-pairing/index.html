THE AUDIT SHELF / FOLLOW THE PAPERS

What comes from the papers—and what does not?

Wang, Xia & Zhao (2011) — signed-ElGamal + BLS and unified verificationIACR ePrint 2011/182. Source for the signed-ElGamal design and symmetric-pairing OR relations. Our Type-3 version is an adaptation, not the paper's original instantiation.
Fubiao Xia (2013) — confirmation/disavowal diagrams and security definitionsUniversity of Birmingham thesis, Chapter 5. Printed disavowal β expressions appear inconsistent with its C=Dˣ relation. Our inequality proof is clearly identified as a candidate reconstruction.
Xia et al. (2016) — a different full-verification constructionTheoretical Computer Science 618. Commitment + labeled encryption construction. Kept separate: this workbench does not claim to implement the 2016 construction.
Camenisch & Shoup (2003) — verifiable decryption and OR invalidity proofsAuthor-hosted paper, §7.3. Separate exact-range/OR building blocks, not interchangeable with this 2013 scheme.
Research boundaries and next verification gates

The browser generates real cryptographic group elements, but that alone does not establish complete zero knowledge or non-transferability.
